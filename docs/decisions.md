# Decision log

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — DECISION — why — REJECTED <option>: <why not> — <link>`. Log in the same turn the choice is made. The trigger is a choice between options (an alternative rejected, a constraint accepted), not a milestone. Mark `AGREED-PENDING-VALIDATION` until a gating check passes. A contested decision may add a full entry (context · options · trade-off · revisit-if) below the one-liners; superseded full entries move to `docs/archive/decisions-YYYY.md`.
